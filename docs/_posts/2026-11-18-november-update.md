# November 2026: 0.75 is nearly here

Halo 3 now plays start to finish on Linux and Windows, and installing it
takes nothing but your own disc image: the launcher builds the game from it
on your PC in a few minutes.

## What's new

- **The installer.** Choose your Halo 3 disc image and the launcher checks
  it, copies the game's files, translates its code and compiles it, with
  the compiler included in the release (on Windows, Microsoft's runtime is
  downloaded once, after you accept its terms). Nothing from Halo 3 is in
  the download.
- **PC controls.** Mouse look straight from the mouse like MCC's, with
  zoomed aiming that slows with the magnification; a mouse pointer in every
  menu; your keys shown in the game's own prompts; toggle crouch and hold
  to zoom; field of view and a centered crosshair.
- **Split screen** for up to four players, and **system link** across a LAN
  or the internet, including between Linux and Windows.
- **60 Hz,** with more fixes for things the 360 tied to 30 ticks (a weapon
  switch animation that popped while moving was the latest).

## Next

The release itself, after testing on real Windows machines; then the native
port: rewriting the recompiled code as readable C++, one subsystem at a time.
